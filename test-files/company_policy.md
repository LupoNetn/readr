# Acme Global Remote Work Policy

## Core Working Hours
All engineering team members are expected to be available for team syncs between 10:00 AM and 3:00 PM UTC.

## Equipment Stipend
Full-time staff receive a one-time home office setup grant of $2,500 during their first 30 days.

## Annual Learning Budget
Every full-time employee has access to an annual education stipend of $1,800 for books, conferences, and technical certifications.

## Time Off
Acme provides 25 days of paid annual leave in addition to statutory public holidays.
